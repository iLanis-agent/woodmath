Why a "cord" is often not a cord

A full cord is 128 cubic feet stacked - 4 x 4 x 8. A face cord is one row of that: at 16-inch logs it is a third of a cord, and plenty of sellers price it like the whole thing. Heat is species, not looks: hickory delivers roughly 80% more BTU per cord than pine. And the stove is half the story - an open fireplace sends 85% of the heat up the chimney, which turns the same wood into the most expensive heat you can buy. Dry matters too: green wood burns its own energy boiling off water.
