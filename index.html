WoodMath

A "cord" is often not a cord. Price the wood you are actually getting, in heat you will actually feel.

The volume lie

A full cord is 128 cubic feet. A face cord at 16-inch logs is a third of that. A store bundle is 0.75 cubic feet - at typical prices, over ten times the cord rate per BTU.

The species truth

Heat is species, not looks. Hickory delivers about 80% more BTU per cord than pine, and oak needs two full years to season while ash burns in six months.

The stove truth

An open fireplace sends 85% of the heat up the chimney. The same cord in an EPA stove costs a fraction per degree - the stove is half the price of wood heat.
